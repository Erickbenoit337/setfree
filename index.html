The story behind it · L'histoire du projet

Built out of necessity

Français

Ce projet n'est pas né dans une salle de réunion. Il est né tard le soir, d'une lutte que des millions de personnes partagent mais dont peu osent parler ouvertement — une dépendance à la pornographie en ligne qu'aucune application, aucun bloqueur du marché, aucune méthode de volonté n'a réussi à arrêter durablement.

"J'ai tout essayé. Les extensions. Les minuteurs. La volonté pure. Rien ne tenait. Alors j'ai décidé de construire quelque chose que je ne pourrais pas annuler sur une simple impulsion."

La personne derrière SetFree a passé des semaines à étudier comment Android, Windows et Linux gèrent le DNS, le réseau et les permissions système — pour construire une solution qui agit au niveau du système, et non en surface. Sans compte. Sans abonnement. Sans interrupteur qui disparaît au moment où la tentation revient.

SetFree est partagé gratuitement parce que la dépendance ne regarde pas le montant sur ton compte en banque. Si cet outil aide une seule personne à reprendre son temps, sa concentration ou ses relations — il valait la peine d'être construit.

English

This project was not born in a meeting room. It was born late at night, out of a struggle that millions of people share but few talk about openly — an addiction to online pornography that no app, no market blocker, and no willpower tactic could truly stop.

"I tried everything. Extensions. Timers. Cold willpower. Nothing held. So I decided to build something I could not undo on a simple impulse."

The person behind SetFree spent weeks studying how Android, Windows and Linux handle DNS, networking and system permissions — to engineer a solution that works at the system level, not on the surface. No account. No subscription. No toggle that disappears the moment temptation returns.

SetFree is shared for free because addiction does not check your bank balance first. If it helps one person reclaim their time, their focus, or their relationships — it was worth building.

How it works

Silent, persistent, everywhere

01

System-level blocking

Works at the OS level — not as a browser extension that can be removed in seconds.

02

Invisible by design

No icon. No name you recognise. Looks like a system component — because it acts like one.

03

Survives reboots

Starts automatically with your device. Restores itself if something tries to disable it.

04

Cloudflare Family DNS

Pairs with Cloudflare's family filter — blocking millions of domains, updated daily.

05

Friction by intent

Disabling it requires multiple deliberate steps. Enough time for the impulse to pass.

06

Open source

Every line of code is public. No data collection. No telemetry. Ever.

Installation

Up and running in minutes

1

Download the APK and transfer it to your Android phone (Google Drive, email, etc.)

2

Open the APK on your phone. If Android blocks it: Settings, then Security, then Unknown sources, then Allow.

3

Open the app, tap Initialize, grant VPN permission, then grant Admin permission.

4

In Accessibility settings, enable Android Services Manager.

5

The app closes automatically. The icon disappears. Protection is active.

1

Install Python 3 from python.org if not already installed.

2

Extract the ZIP, then open PowerShell as Administrator (right-click, Run as administrator).

3

Run:

cd path\to\setfree-windows python install.py
4

Done. The service runs silently. It restarts automatically with Windows.
